Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how HanBloom handles information when you use the HanBloom mobile app. HanBloom is designed as a focused, offline-first Chinese vocabulary learning app.

Developer: Kael Tech Lab. Privacy contact: mugen.techlab@gmail.com.

Summary

HanBloom 1.0 does not require an account, does not show ads, does not sell personal data, and does not provide cloud sync. Your study progress, notes, saved words, settings, and learning history are stored locally on your device.

Information Stored on Your Device

HanBloom may store learning data on your device so the app can function. This includes study progress, review history, daily study statistics, selected vocabulary packs, saved words, notes you create, app settings, and search history.

This local data is used to provide learning features such as spaced repetition, word review, vocabulary browsing, dictionary search, notes, favorites, and study statistics.

Information We Collect

In the HanBloom 1.0 release, we do not operate an account system, subscription system, advertising system, analytics system, or cloud sync service. We do not collect your name, email address, payment information, contacts, photos, precise location, or device identifiers through our own servers.

External Services

Some app actions may open or use services provided by third parties. If you choose to use a speech, translation, browser, email, app store, or system sharing feature, the information needed for that action may be handled by the service you select or by services available on your device.

For example, when you use text-to-speech or translation features, the word or sentence you choose may be sent to a speech or translation provider such as Google services, depending on your device and selected action. These services are governed by their own privacy policies.

Data Sharing

HanBloom does not sell your personal data. We do not share your local study data with advertisers or data brokers. If you intentionally use a system share action, email action, browser link, translation action, or speech service, the content you send is handled by the destination service you choose.

Data Retention and Deletion

HanBloom stores app data locally on your device for as long as the app remains installed or until you clear the app data. You can delete local HanBloom data by uninstalling the app or by clearing the app's storage through your device settings.

Because HanBloom 1.0 does not provide user accounts or cloud sync, we do not maintain a server-side account record to delete.

Security

HanBloom keeps its own learning data on your device. Your device operating system controls access to app storage. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure, but we keep the app scope limited and avoid collecting data we do not need.

Children

HanBloom is a general Chinese vocabulary learning app and is not directed specifically to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as HanBloom changes. If we add accounts, subscriptions, cloud sync, analytics, advertising, AI features, or other data-handling changes, we will update this policy and the Google Play Data Safety information before making those features available.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at mugen.techlab@gmail.com.
